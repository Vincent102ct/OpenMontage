2026-10-23. Branch `coding`, baseline HEAD `ba94c70f45e52032d318693c88ac41454e803994`.
Reusable-asset foundation only: synthetic demonstration material, no real pilot,

## Architecture

No second animation engine. Every asset resolves into the existing `story_video`
contract and renders through `character_rig_renderer → video_compose → Remotion`
with the shared `motion.ts` two-bone IK, seekable tracks and `walk_clip`.

| Layer | Source | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registry | `tools/character/horror_assets.py` | Stable `id` + integer `revision` for rig, character, silhouette, prop, environment, acting and lighting assets; content-hash manifest; pinned bindings. |
| Environments | `tools/character/horror_environments.py` | Procedural SVG backdrops with explicit world-space anchors. |
| Acting presets | `tools/character/horror_acting.py` | `ActorBuilder`: deterministic keyframes over existing tracks. |
| Cast artwork | `tools/character/remotion/CartoonCast.tsx` | Draws a character from its design data over the shared rig state. |
| Location lighting | `tools/character/remotion/CinematicHorror.tsx` (`HorrorOverlay`) | Overlay driven by per-location geometry. |
| Validation | `tools/character/story_video.py` (`_check_geometry`) | Fail-closed checks for geometry, drivers and tracks. |
| Demo | `tools/character/asset_library_demo.py`, `scripts/p32a_*.py` | Two synthetic stories, reference reels and QA. |

Opt-in surfaces (absent in every existing package):

- `look.design` (with `character_skin: "cartoon_horror"`) selects `CastBody`/`CastHands`.
  Without it, `cartoon_horror` still draws P3.1 `CartoonHorror`; `cartoon` and
  `stickman_horror` are unchanged.
- `location.cinematic` selects the location-aware `HorrorOverlay`. Locations without
  it keep the exact P3.1 v2 overlay when `horror` tracks exist, or plain `StoryVideo`.
- `horror.flicker` is an optional `[t, intensity]` track for flicker-driven lights.

Composition IDs, `StoryVideoProps` fields, rig bone lengths and the fiction/story/
production-request schemas are unchanged. `stickman_horror_2d` Studio identifiers stay as-is.

## Registry format

`horror_assets.manifest()` (library `openmontage.cinematic_cartoon_horror`, version
`p3.2a.1`) lists 18 assets as `{kind, id, revision, summary, sha256}`. The hash is
over the asset's canonical JSON (environments: their resolved SVG and anchors), so
editing data without a new revision is detectable. `get(kind, id, revision)` raises
for unknown IDs or a revision the library does not hold; there is no silent upgrade.
`REVISION_HASHES` stores literal fingerprints for all 18 revisions. Both reads and
manifest validation compare content against those stored fingerprints and reject
drift. Reads return defensive copies. Editing the data and its fingerprint together
is a source change requiring review; hashes are an integrity check, not a signature.

Sample character entry (abridged):

```json
{"id": "victor_hale", "revision": 1, "kind": "character", "rig": "biped_v1",
 "rest_posture": [8, -96, 15, -157],
 "design": {"palette": {"skin": "#a87e64", "hair": "#8f8b84", "top": "#5a4e34", "...": "..."},
            "build": {"shoulder": 27, "hip": 29, "sleeve": 22, "leg": 23, "head": 1.04, "hand": 1.1},
            "outfit": "cardigan", "footwear": "shoes",
            "face": {"outline": "M -31 -18 Q ...", "eyes": {"shape": "round", "...": "..."}, "...": "..."},
            "hair": {"front": "M -34 6 L ..."}, "features": [{"d": "glasses ...", "stroke": "#202426"}]}}
```

`check_library()` rejects a rig whose bones differ from the shared IK, non-`#rrggbb`
palettes, unreachable rest postures and unsafe head scale/placement. Body
proportions vary only through drawing widths, head scale and rest posture; skeleton
bone lengths stay fixed so the existing evaluator and validation remain valid.

## Environments and anchors

`old_apartment_room@1` and `dark_apartment_corridor@1` return `{svg, door, window,
features}`. `features` exposes `floor_y` (620), `walk_range`, `doors[]` (rect,
`handle` = `doorHandle()` twin, `seam`), `windows[]` (rect + four pane polygons
between StoryVideo's mullions), `lamps[]`, `presence_anchors` (window panes or the
corridor's dark opening, clip polygons) and named `props` points (switch, table,
radiator, notice board, armchair). The door leaf, handle and glass are still drawn
by `StoryVideo` from the same rects, so anchors stay aligned under any camera move.

## Location-aware lighting

`lighting_geometry(env, preset, flicker_lamp=, presence=)` builds `location.cinematic`
from the environment's own features. Presets:

- `cold_moonlight`: needs a window; beam and pool from the window rect.
- `dim_corridor`: needs lamps; cone, glow and pool per lamp.
- `flickering_interior`: needs lamps; one lamp is driven by `horror.flicker`.

All presets add door-seam spill (driven by `horror.spill`). A preset whose required
feature is missing raises; there is no fallback to the P3.1 room. The pool mask
is Gaussian-blurred, because hard pool edges rendered as rounded dark columns that
read as unintended figures.

Silhouettes (`featureless_figure@1`, `tall_hooded@1`) are placed only when a
binding requests a presence anchor. A story without one draws no figure.

Validation fails on unknown or extra geometry keys, a version other than 1, points
outside 1280×720, non-finite or out-of-range numbers, bad colors, unknown drivers,
the flicker driver without a `horror.flicker` track, clip polygons with fewer than
three points, and geometry without `horror` tracks.

Documented safe fallback: a location with no `cinematic` key keeps the unchanged
P3.1 v2 overlay. This exists only so saved P3.1 packages render identically; library
bindings always emit explicit geometry.

## Acting presets

`cautious_walk`, `startled_reaction`, `listening_freeze` and `guarded_reach` append
keys to one current pose in time order.

- **Walk:** reuses `walk_clip`, limits the step period so the trailing foot stays
  inside leg reach, and rejects speeds over 55 px/s as "not cautious".
- **Listening freeze:** locks root and feet exactly.
- **Reach:** checks the anchor is reachable from the stance before emitting keys.

Presets do not guarantee a convincing performance; the renders still need review.

## Story binding (minimum for a future Story Visual Bible)

```json
{"story_id": "synthetic-lamp-four", "library": "openmontage.cinematic_cartoon_horror",
 "library_version": "p3.2a.1",
 "cast": {"resident": {"character": "victor_hale", "revision": 1}},
 "locations": {"hall": {"environment": "dark_apartment_corridor", "revision": 1,
   "lighting": {"id": "flickering_interior", "revision": 1, "flicker_lamp": "lamp_mid"},
   "presence": {"anchor": "corridor_end", "silhouette": "tall_hooded", "revision": 1}}}}
```

Required:

- Story ID and the exact library/version.
- Per role, a pinned `character@revision`. One character asset may not play two roles.
- Per location key, a pinned `environment@revision` and `lighting@revision`, with
  optional flicker lamp and optional presence anchor/silhouette.

`validate_binding` rejects missing or extra keys, unknown revisions and unknown
anchors. `resolve_binding` produces identical `look` objects for every shot of a role.
Revision pins must be positive integers (not null, bool, float or string), including
lighting and silhouette pins. Nested lighting/presence keys and environment-feature
compatibility are validated before resolution. For opted-in `asset_binding` videos,
the renderer also rejects appearance, costume, anchor or cinematic-geometry drift
from the pinned assets before writing a workspace. Legacy videos without a binding
never consult this registry.

Deliberately out of scope for P3.2A: wardrobe changes, ageing/injury states, per-scene
costume continuity, casting rules, and links to Master Story Package
characters/claims. No canonical fiction or historical schema was changed.

## Known limitations (unresolved)

- Bent-knee/stylized walking and guarded hands inherited from P3.1. The rest pose reads as hands
  on hips; there are no articulated fingers, so the final contact covers the handle.
- One three-quarter view per character; look-backs use eyes and tilt only.
- Victor's stubble, face shade and directional shadow stack into a muddy lower face.
- Lighting is authored vector shading: planar beams, no cast body shadows. Flicker
  only modulates lamp glow, cones and pools; character shading does not change.
- The presence silhouette is a flat shape; a visible hooded mass, not a crafted reveal.
- Shot A2 resumes nearer the door than shot A1 ends: the cut deliberately elides time.
- The startle reaction moves hands quickly (17.5 units in one frame).
- Synthetic Foley; the listening window in story A holds room tone only.
- No automated aesthetic score. Fear, acting quality and listening require human review.
