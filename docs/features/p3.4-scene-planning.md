# P3.4 — Canonical Story-to-Scene Planner + Studio review

2026-10-23. Implemented on `coding` from baseline
`0f4cfba53b4ea1323db3e1886c72ac9f968baed9`; **pending user review**. Nothing is
staged, committed or pushed. P3.3 remains the accepted visual-design foundation.
Synthetic approvals below are test actions, not approval of a real story or of
artistic/audio quality.

## What P3.4 does

It turns a complete approved fiction story into structured, source-traceable
Short (9:16) and Long (16:9) scene plans: chapters → scenes → ordered shots, each
with canonical references, narration and attribution, dialogue/sound cues,
blocking, performance, camera, lighting, prop interactions, continuity before and
after, a provisional timing estimate, and an honest capability verdict.

It never reinterprets the story. Chapters, beats, narration text/spans and
canonical IDs come only from the approved reviewed format plans. No keyword
analyzer reads the story; `tools/story/story_analyzer.py` and
`tools/story/story_planner.py` are untouched. No event, identity, dialogue or
supernatural explanation is invented.

## Verified inputs

Preparation requires both, re-verified at call time:

1. The **current** explicitly approved P2.3 Reviewed Fiction Story Package
   (`studio.reviewed.read_reviewed_story`, `ready_for_next_stage`).
2. The **current** explicitly approved P3.3 Story Visual Bible for that canon,
   obtained via the verified `studio.visual_bible.scene_planning_handoff`.

`inputs` pins: reviewed version, source/working-text/package/manifest hashes,
approval **file** hash and decision-history digest (the P3.3 `canonical_ref`);
Visual Bible version, package/manifest hashes, approval file hash and handoff
digest; library `openmontage.cinematic_cartoon_horror@p3.2a.1` manifest hash; every
selected asset `{kind,id,revision,sha256}`; and the four P3.2A acting presets.
Reading any version re-verifies both pinned inputs and their own histories, then
recompiles byte-for-byte. A changed asset revision, story decision or newer Visual
Bible makes the plan stale; tampering is refused without overwriting.

`studio/visual_bible.py` gained one helper, `handoff_from_view`, so historical
verification rebuilds the exact handoff of a pinned version. Behaviour of
`scene_planning_handoff` is unchanged. Historical/Master Story canon has no
approval adapter and stays out of scope; STORY 053 and the real pilot are not read.

## Contract and versioning

Schema `schemas/story/story_scene_plan.schema.json`, version `1.0`.
Compiler `lib/story_scene_plan.py`; storage `studio/scene_plan.py`, reusing P2.3
`review_lock`, `safe_review_path`, `write_once`, `_record_hash` and atomic folder
publication.

`artifacts/story_scene_plan/v1/`, then v2, v3, …:

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `scene_plan.json` | Immutable candidate: pins, both formats, coverage, continuity, retiming contract, warnings, blockers. `review_status` stays `pending_explicit_scene_plan_approval`. |
| `manifest.json` | Exact inputs, structured staging choices, package hash, previous-manifest hash. |
| `approval.json` | Added once by a separate explicit action: exact hashes, `approval_scope: scene_plan_only`, TTS/rendering/production/publishing all false, integrity hash. |

Shot and scene IDs (`SH_L_007`, `SC_S_02`) derive only from canon, so they are
stable across staging revisions. Changing choices creates a new version; preparing
against new inputs creates a version that carries still-valid staging forward but
clears every review/acknowledgement. Identical saves/approvals are idempotent.

### Source facts vs staging proposals

Each shot has `source` (read-only: beat, chapter, events, characters, location,
objects, sound cues, narration text/spans/working spans and attribution,
dialogue overlapping the narration span, facts, related ambiguities and
continuity rules, the reviewed staging text and silence) and `staging`
(`creative_proposal_not_canonical_fact`). The reviewed staging text is kept as a
`descriptive_proposal` with `executable: false`; it is never compiled into motion.

## Coverage

- Every reviewed beat gets exactly one narrative shot, in order; beats with sound
  cues or a reveal and ≥1.5 s planned silence also get a reaction-hold shot.
- An independent check (`coverage()`) recomputes, from the plan's own shots:
  missing/duplicated/re-ordered beats, any changed source field, chapter/scene/shot
  ordering, orphan reaction shots; for Long, every non-whitespace character of the
  retained reviewed text, every canonical event, sound cue and dialogue line; for
  Short, that its sources and events are within Long. Failures are blockers.
- Short target 45–60 s is reported, never enforced: nothing is padded, cut or
  rewritten. Long has no duration target and is never truncated.
- Unsupported animation never removes a beat; it becomes a blocker.

## Direction, capability and the executable boundary

Staging comes from a closed vocabulary (`vocabulary()`), each entry with a status:
`supported_direct`, `supported_parameterized`, `editorial_review`,
`requires_new_capability`.

- Defaults are structural, not textual: listening freeze when the beat has sound
  cues or a reveal, otherwise hold; partial silhouette only where the beat lists
  that canonical partial character and the Visual Bible binds a presence anchor;
  flicker only with `flickering_interior` and an electronic cue; marks from the
  pinned environment walk range and door-handle anchors; entry by
  `present_at_cut`, `revealed_by_framing` (only if canon already placed the person
  there) or `enter_walk`.
- On-screen speech for a canonical speaker defaults to `on_screen_speech`, which is
  `requires_new_capability` (no lip-sync). Voice-over or off-screen delivery is an
  explicit editorial choice.
- Unsupported: `open_door`, `open_window`, `grasp_object`, `turn_around`, `run`,
  `handheld_drift`, `dolly_zoom`, sustained `blackout`. Editorial:
  `reach_then_opened_across_cut`, `door_opened/closed_across_cut`,
  `offscreen_action`, voice-over delivery. Editorial items block approval until the
  shot's editorial acknowledgement is checked.
- Every executable step is dry-run through the real P3.2A `ActorBuilder`
  (`cautious_walk` ≤ 45 px/s, `guarded_reach` to the pinned anchor point,
  `listening_freeze`, `startled_reaction`, `hold`). A failure is a
  `MOTION_PLAN_INVALID` blocker. Camera keys `[t, cx, cy, zoom]` are checked
  against the same artwork bounds StoryVideo enforces; subjects that do not fit the
  9:16 or 16:9 width are reported as framed out, not silently cropped.

## Continuity

A state machine per format tracks character location/mark/x/facing/visibility/
performance/emotion, prop location/anchor/state (door open/closed; lamps follow
each shot's lighting), lighting per location, partial-presence state and
canonical uncertainty. Each shot stores `before`/`after`.

Blockers: a location change marked `continuous` (`ACCIDENTAL_TELEPORT`), a person
appearing mid-scene without entry or not established there (`TELEPORT`),
re-entering while visible, persistent prop states that differ between Short and
Long after a shared event (`FORMAT_CONTINUITY`), changed canonical uncertainty and
differing Short/Long identity digests. Repositioning across an intentional cut and
facing flips across cuts are recorded as notes.

## Timing and the P3.5 handoff

Timing is `provisional_estimate`: the existing P2.2 basis (syllables / 4.35 per
second, measured on Vietnamese narration) plus lead 0.3 s / tail 0.4 s, planned
silence and action minimums, quantized to 30 fps. Non-Vietnamese stories are
flagged low-confidence. No WAV, audio path or measurement report is produced.

The immutable `retiming_contract` in existing scene-plan versions keeps its
original bytes. The repaired **handoff contract 1.1** adds a separate
`measurement_contract` per format, with an ordered unit/shot/text-hash inventory
and exact project/version/package/manifest/input pins. The strict envelope schema
is `$defs/measurement_set` in `story_scene_plan.schema.json`. Measurements include
provenance and a canonical-JSON manifest fingerprint. Wrong order, duplicate or
missing units, stale pins, invalid durations and malformed hashes fail closed.

`retime(plan, format, measurement_set, approved=verified_view)` returns a new
in-memory derivative. The storage consumer `retime_scene_plan` re-reads current
canon, Visual Bible and scene-plan approval under the existing review lock, then
stores an idempotent, write-once derivative under `artifacts/scene_plan_retiming/`.
It never changes approved scene-plan files. A previously read Python view alone
cannot establish current disk state; consumers must use this storage boundary.

The conservative timing policy preserves approved shot durations, action lengths,
reaction silence and camera cues as minimums. It rounds action durations upward
to 30fps without speeding them up, extends narrative shots for longer narration
or actions, reflows scene/chapter/final boundaries, and holds final camera framing
when a shot grows. It does not shorten an approved staging hold to hit a target.
An action that cannot fit a fixed reaction hold is a structured blocker requiring
new reviewed staging. Narration stays inside its shot with the planned tail and
silence. This is a timing contract, not a renderer implementation.

`action_timeline()` rejects partial timing lists or mutated derivatives. The
existing action-timeline schema remains unchanged; its metadata carries the full
approved format (including source, narration, dialogue, prop anchors, camera and
lighting intents, capabilities and continuity), input/approval binding, resolved
frame timings and narration/action links. `validate_action_timeline` checks the
post-conversion inventory and semantics against the approved plan;
`handoff_coverage` independently recomputes both formats' canonical coverage from
the converted payload. Unsupported capabilities produce structured errors and
never remove a shot or trigger a generic fallback.

Every output remains **`renderable: false`**, including retimed derivatives.
`synthetic_test` measurements carry null audio metadata and are labeled
`synthetic_test_not_measured_audio`; they are not WAV/TTS evidence. Real-audio
declarations require content hashes and sample rates but remain
`declared_measured_audio_pending_verification`: P3.4 does not verify or generate
audio files. `story_planner.plan_story(analysis, narration)` is unchanged and is
not fed estimates. TTS, actual audio verification and compilation into
`story_video` remain P3.5+ work.

## Studio workflow

**My Productions → Scene Plans** (or the section below Visual Bible):

1. Approve the story (P2.3) and Visual Bible (P3.3).
2. **Prepare Scene Plans** saves an unapproved immutable v1.
3. Inspect Short/Long chapters, scenes and shots: narration and source spans,
   events/characters/objects/sounds/dialogue, reviewed staging (not executable),
   camera keys and framing, cast blocking, capability badges/items, continuity
   notes, executable motion steps, blockers and the coverage report.
4. Change shot size, camera, lighting state, partial presence, performance,
   emotion, mark, entry, prop interaction, dialogue delivery or scene transition.
   Narration, beats and canonical facts are not editable. Changes clear that
   scene's review; **Save new scene-plan version**.
5. Acknowledge editorial representations, review each scene, coverage and
   continuity; save again.
6. With zero blockers, check the separate consent and **Approve Scene Plans**. The
   POST pins both displayed hashes; reloaded or newer inputs invalidate old tabs.

API under `/studio/api/productions/{id}`: GET `/scene-plan`, `/scene-plan/{v}`,
`/scene-plan/{v}/report`, `/scene-plan/handoff`; POST `/scene-plan` (`{}` or base
version + hashes + choices) and `/scene-plan/{v}/approve`. Existing local-origin,
JSON size, trusted-host and no-store protections apply. No route triggers TTS,
rendering, checkpoints or publishing.

## Known limitations and remaining blockers before full-story rendering

- Measured narration (P3.5), voice choice and listening review are absent.
- No lip-sync, door-leaf/hand coupling, finger grasp, body turns, running or
  window opening; those beats rely on editorial representations or new
  capabilities. Two identities, two environments and four props remain the
  library limit; more cast/locations need new assets.
- Structural checks cannot judge whether a proposal is cinematically good or
  semantically faithful; humans review each scene.
- Compilation of the IR into `story_video`, Foley/audio mix and rendering are not
  implemented. No full-story render was produced.
- Verification recompiles each version from its pinned inputs, as P3.3 does. A
  future planner change that alters output makes existing versions fail
  verification (refused, never overwritten), so such changes need an explicit
  compatibility/version decision.
