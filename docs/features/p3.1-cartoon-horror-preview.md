## Change and reuse boundary

`character_skin: "cartoon_horror"` selects cel-style SVG artwork: filled hoodie,
trousers, sneakers, tousled hair, skin, separate eyes/pupils, eyebrows, gasp/frown
and hands. Clothing follows the evaluated shoulder/hip and hands follow the
evaluated wrists. Artwork uses the existing two-bone IK, seekable tracks,
`walk_clip`, `StoryVideo`, `character_rig_renderer` handoff and native Remotion
audio/render path. No independent animation solver, raster/video generation,
external assets, narration or model download is added.

`CartoonHorror.tsx` is selected in the shared Actor entry points; the old cartoon
default and explicit stickman mode remain compatible. Python validates optional
posture tracks and uses their actual shoulders when checking limb reach, matching
the existing TypeScript evaluator. No existing story is automatically restyled.

The synthetic P3.1 fixture derives only mechanical tracks from the P3.0 synthetic
fixture. It supplies its own original SVG room, costume colors, face/posture
keys, camera and final reach. Cold window light, a crooked portrait, damaged
walls, dark panels and a restrained warm door seam keep the horror setting.
The exposure keys stay above the shared window's .15 day/night threshold to
avoid a hard color switch. Camera moves 13 world units right, 15 down and zooms
7% over ten seconds. Feet settle before the hand reaches the closed door handle.

The generic preview runner accepts a project ID, fixture builder, frame checker
and QA timestamps. P3.0 defaults are retained; P3.1 writes exclusively to its
own ignored project and composer staging directories. Existing scope-marker and
gitignore checks prevent overwriting an unrelated project.

Preflight registry reported FFmpeg, Remotion and HyperFrames available, and 6/6
character-animation tools configured. The user explicitly selected the existing
Remotion route. HyperFrames could author HTML/SVG scenes but would require a
different handoff for this existing React rig; FFmpeg handles SFX/decode/QA.
No paid APIs are called. The local `character-animation` pipeline remains beta.

## Reproduce on this Windows checkout

```powershell

$env:REMOTION_BROWSER_EXECUTABLE = 'C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe'
```

## Visual review and remaining defects

Actual final contact sheet, raised-foot walking frame, reaction and final reach
were inspected. Hair, clothing, eyes, gasp, bent-knee caution and reach read as
a filled cartoon character. No detached joints or hidden primary action observed.
Key-frame times: 0, 1.2, 1.8, 3.2, 5.067, 6.4, 8.4 and 9.9 seconds.

- Mitten hands cover the small handle at contact. Fingers do not articulate;
  contact may read as a touch/knock, not a full grasp. Door stays closed.
- The inherited walk is a stylized bent-knee shuffle. Clothing/shoes have no
  physical simulation; no claim of realistic gait or weight transfer.
- One three-quarter character view; gaze/tilt convey the turn. There is no
  independently drawn profile/back view, volumetric relighting or cast body shadow.
- Upper wall space is generous; room detail and light beams remain procedural
  vector art. The under-door warm seam is subtle at contact-sheet size.
- Synthesized scrape/footsteps need subjective listening approval.
- Studio's historical style identifiers/UI labels are unchanged; schema/intake
  migration and saved package conversion are outside this renderer sample.

These limits do not block technical preview delivery. Human artistic acceptance,
audio listening, real-pilot integration and production/publication remain pending.
