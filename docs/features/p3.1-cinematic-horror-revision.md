## Extension of P3.1

The first preview and its entire project directory remain byte-identical at
`projects/p31-cartoon-horror-preview/`. The new version lives separately in
`projects/p31-cartoon-horror-preview-v2/`. Existing `build_preview` and the original
CLI remain available. `build_cinematic_preview` derives from that fixture and
changes only the synthetic scene's direction, acting, light and sound tracks.

The primary visual identity is Cinematic 2D Cartoon Supernatural Horror: mature
characters, directional low-key light, negative fill, an enclosed interior,
guarded fear acting and a partially obscured presence. No gore, monster insert,
cut/stinger jump scare, explanatory text or slideshow is used.

`look.treatment: "cinematic"` opts the existing `cartoon_horror` artwork into a
narrower angular face, almond eyes, restrained parted lips, directional face and
costume shadows, muted color and guarded hands. The old P3.1 artwork remains
identical at four SVG fixtures when this option is absent. Default cartoon and
legacy Stickman remain available and pass the three historical SVG fixtures.
The new preview uses `cartoon_horror`, never Stickman.

`DirectedStoryVideo` wraps the existing `StoryVideo` only when optional `horror`
tracks are supplied with the cartoon skin. It draws a world-aligned negative-fill
mask, foreground doorway, low light beam, window-pane occlusion, faint haze and
tracked light spill/presence. The existing composition ID and metadata contract
remain unchanged. Without these tracks it renders the existing component.
No new renderer, IK solver, motion clock or external asset provider is added.

The 30 unrelated dirty files observed at revision start remain byte-identical,
including existing `motion.ts`, `StoryVideo.tsx` and `video_compose.py` WIP.
The brief referred to 28; verification conservatively preserved all 30 observed.
The new wrapper avoids editing the existing world/posture WIP. Runtime remains
`character_rig_renderer → video_compose → Remotion`, with local Chrome and FFmpeg.

## Dramatic beats

| Time | Acting, atmosphere and sound |
| --- | --- |
| 0–1.2 s | Closer listening composition; room tone, visible lit face, partly offscreen door. |
| 1.2–2.1 s | Scratch, quick shoulder tension, hands drawn inward, head/eye reaction; door spill falls. |
| 2.3–3.5 s | Measured silence; character listens before moving. |
| 4–6.2 s | Guarded planted-foot approach. |
| 6.1–6.85 s | Quiet creak and dimmer spill; walking freezes at 6.2 s, gaze turns toward the window; partially obscured silhouette eases in and holds unnaturally still. |
| 6.9–8.4 s | Movement resumes; silhouette fades by 7.25 s without an audio stinger. |
| 8.4–9.35 s | Feet settle; hesitant hand motion, then reach. |
| 9.35–10 s | Wrist holds the actual closed-door handle; silence leaves the outcome unresolved. |

Camera begins closer on the listener, with the door intentionally cropped during
the early offscreen-sound beat. It follows the approach slowly and reveals the
door/contact clearly by the final beat. Unlike the wide first preview, it uses
the portrait frame's height and reduces the empty upper wall. The evaluator
still requires the entire actor/artwork inside the safe area, including sleeve
thickness, and verifies that contact is in frame. The legacy whole-door framing
test remains the default; cinematic QA explicitly permits early door cropping.

## Remaining defects and limits

- The inherited bent-knee gait remains a stylized shuffle. The resumed approach
  is brisk relative to its listening hold; weight transfer and cloth are not simulated.
- Hands have a guarded/open outline, not independently animated fingers. Final
  contact partly covers the handle and can read as a touch rather than a grasp.
- Eyes/tilt carry the look-back; no separate profile/back drawing or full torso turn.
- Lighting is authored cel shading and SVG compositing, not physically computed
  illumination. The beam can look like a planar band; no full cast-body shadow.
- The window figure is a simple featureless silhouette. Its movement/hold is
  restrained, but painterly texture, refraction and a richer reveal need more art work.
- Procedural room/props and Foley remain visibly/audibly synthetic. Listening,
  subjective fear, facial nuance and final artistic acceptance require human review.
- Studio's historical request identifiers/UI labels remain unchanged for compatibility.
