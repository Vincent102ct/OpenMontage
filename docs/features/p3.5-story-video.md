# P3.5 synthetic full-story integration

P3.5 adapts current, verified P2.3 reviewed fiction, P3.3 Visual Bible and P3.4 Scene
Plans to the existing StoryVideo/ActorBuilder/IK/Remotion contracts. It adds no
animation engine and does not modify the measured-audio legacy story_planner API.
Technical proof and human artistic/audio acceptance are separate.

## Visual fidelity repair: separate execution review

The initial English proof exposed two semantic gaps: `flickering_interior` drives
the unselected lamps as constant lights, and generic hold/listening starts from
a standing pose. Narration/source coverage alone did not verify those pixels.
The previous reports, MP4s, approvals and measured WAVs remain preserved.

The opt-in `scene_visual_staging/1.0` contract records per-shot lamp tracks,
seated-at-cut actors, verified chair anchors, framing, exact segment quotes and
explicit cut/time-ellipsis choices. It binds the exact Scene Plan, measured timing,
narration set and capability implementation hashes. It neither edits nor migrates
P3.3/P3.4 packages or pinned asset revision data. Changes create new immutable
candidate versions; every affected shot/cut needs a separate explicit Studio
review and hash-bound approval. Updating renderer code never grants that approval.

New full-story render requests additionally require the approved current staging
reference, matching measured narration and separate synthetic render consent.
Pending/stale candidates cannot start a render. Original legacy drafts remain
historical evidence. Studio offers before/after state, source evidence, individual
review checkboxes, separate approval consent and an immutable candidate editor.

Per-lamp emission overrides use the verified geometry-to-fixture binding for glow,
beam and floor pool together. OFF fixtures remain in the original environment SVG.
Keys can hold a real zero intensity; no dark cover rectangle or asset-hash change.
Legacy packages without this state retain their original drivers and flicker dips.

`ActorBuilder.seated_listening` places the pelvis at the chair seat, bends the
existing 57/58px IK legs, plants feet on the floor and preserves listening contact.
Its opt-in sit-down transition requires a reviewed planted stance and >=1.2s;
it never teleports a distant actor to the chair. Legacy standing/walk/reach methods
are unchanged. Any relocation from a seated hold across a cut needs explicit
time-ellipsis review. Walk/reach steps cannot be removed by a seated override.

Focused capability MP4s are isolated synthetic experiments. They do not approve
the repaired story staging or replace the required complete renders after approval.
The new candidate and repair report live under `projects/p35f-20261026/`.

## Eligibility and authorization

Only the exact SHA-256 digests of the authored English P3.4 fixture and independently
authored Vietnamese P3.5 fixture are eligible. A client `approved` or `synthetic`
flag cannot enable production. Current verified Studio reads validate source,
approval, Visual Bible, planner version and pinned asset revisions before work,
before publishing audio, between formats and before publishing a result.

Synthetic test approvals are automated fixture setup, never evidence of real human
artistic approval. Real pilot, STORY 053, production approval and publishing remain
outside this milestone. No rendering starts on GET, page load or scene approval.

## Studio workflow

Open a synthetic production in My Productions / Scene Plans. The Local synthetic
story video panel provides offline readiness, matching installed voice selection,
explicit Prepare measured narration, sample-derived timing, a separate unchecked
render consent, and complete Short/Long draft rendering. Background jobs expose
progress and structured failures. Completed current results provide MP4 playback,
QA JSON, complete contact sheets and UTF-8 SRT. Backlot is linked for monitoring.

Run Studio in the same native user environment that can enumerate installed voices.
Restricted Windows sandboxes can deny System.Speech registry/COM access even when
the native shell succeeds. Readiness reports that failure without substitution.
For restricted FFmpeg runners, verified copies and a process-local PATH are allowed;
no machine-specific binary path is embedded in product source.

## Audio and measurement contract

Each exact raw approved narration unit is synthesized with installed language-matched
offline TTS. Mono 24kHz PCM16 files must contain all declared samples and meaningful
non-clipping audio. The bundle records sample count/duration, actual WAV SHA-256,
unit/shot/text hashes, voice/provider/model/settings and exact plan/version/package/
manifest/input hashes. Cache identities include exact plan, format, ordered units
and voice settings. Every cache reuse recomputes audio facts. Corruption fails;
historical files are not overwritten to hide a failure.

Only actual verified samples create measured_audio provenance. P3.4 retime and
action_timeline validate derivatives, cuts, action limits and complete shot order.
Existing approved Scene Plans use P3.4's version/integrity checks unchanged. Older
incompatible plans fail the existing reader; no frozen plan is rewritten or silently
reinterpreted. Generated derivatives and production artifacts are separate.

## Compilation, captions and sound

The adapter preserves every approved shot/source/context, retimed camera, cast,
action, editorial acknowledgement, ambiguity, prop state and anchor. It maps only
structured supported presets to ActorBuilder. Unavailable capability rejects
compilation. Four-decimal ActorBuilder terminal keys are snapped to the exact
retimed boundary in the adapter, without shortening actions or changing the engine.
The flickering lamp is recovered from the pinned Visual Bible geometry.

An immutable UTF-8 format narration derivative joins exact approved units with blank
lines. The legacy renderer normalizes paragraph whitespace; this representation is
explicitly recorded. Raw TTS text and canonical/unit hashes remain authoritative.
Legacy source validation is unchanged. Existing renderer captions use actual
narration-unit windows; SRT is generated from those same windows. These are unit
captions, not ASR-derived word timestamps or lip-sync. Approved voice-over retains
dialogue attribution and editorial context in the compiled approved-format metadata.

Only explicit fixture sound cue IDs map to procedural recipes. Knock cues produce
three quiet knocks; room tone is quiet under narration and omitted from reaction
holds. A low-gain fridge_hum recipe represents electrical buzz. This is an explicit
limited sound treatment for review, not a recording of a real lamp. Silence cues
remain silence. Unknown required sound cues fail closed. No constant loud score.

## Evidence and limits

New outputs live under fresh gitignored `projects/p35i-20261026/` directories. A
clean source snapshot contains HEAD plus P3.5 source, excluding unrelated WIP.
Installed verified FFmpeg/ffprobe copies and existing Node dependencies are used;
no dependency/model download is required. Actual MP4 QA includes stream facts,
complete decode/frame count, audio peaks/audibility, subtitle windows/text, per-shot
frames, motion measurements, contact sheets and the exact final decoded frame.

The run report records exact successful media paths, source/shot coverage, test IDs,
failures and final preservation results. Earlier failed experiments remain evidence
and are excluded from final acceptance. Draft output is half resolution at 30fps,
with complete canonical shot inventories. No artificial truncation or speed-up.

Subjective listening, acting, hands/gait, camera crops, vector lighting, suspense and
sound treatment need human review. Source/PCM validation is not speech recognition:
it cannot prove every word was pronounced correctly. Vietnamese narration evidence
does not imply Vietnamese video acceptance without an actual rendered/reviewed
Vietnamese video. Historic P3.2A anomalies remain changed/unverified. No private
data, historic evidence, unrelated WIP, staged index or remote refs are modified.
