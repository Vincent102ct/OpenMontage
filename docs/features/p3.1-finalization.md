## Product identity

[PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md), [AGENT_GUIDE.md](../../AGENT_GUIDE.md) and
[PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](../../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) establish **Cinematic 2D Cartoon
Supernatural Horror Animation** as the primary visual identity: recognizable
cartoon characters, character-driven horror scenes, atmospheric environments,
cinematic light/camera/sound and consistent identities across Short and Long,
built on existing `StoryVideo`, `Actor`, Remotion and two-bone IK.

Backward compatibility:

- `stickman_horror` remains an optional legacy skin; default `cartoon` and 16:9
  compositions are unchanged. The `StoryVideo` composition ID is unchanged.
- `cartoon_horror`, `look.treatment: "cinematic"`, `posture`, `atmosphere` and
  `horror` tracks are opt-in. `DirectedStoryVideo` renders plain `StoryVideo`
  unless `horror` tracks and the `cartoon_horror` skin are both present.
- Studio/intake/schema identifiers such as `stickman_horror_2d` are not changed.
  Saved production requests, reviewed packages and Story Bibles are not migrated.

## Known limitations (not resolved)

- Inherited bent-knee gait reads as a stylized shuffle; the resumed approach is
  brisk relative to the listening hold. No weight transfer or cloth simulation.
- Hands are guarded/open outlines without articulated fingers; final contact can
  read as a touch rather than a grasp. Door does not open; knob does not rotate.
- One three-quarter view; eyes/tilt carry the look-back. No profile/back drawing.
- Lighting is authored vector cel shading; the beam can read as a planar band and
  there is no full cast body shadow.
- The `DirectedStoryVideo` overlay (light pool, beam, foreground doorway) uses
  world coordinates authored for the synthetic room; other locations need their
  own geometry before reuse.
- The window figure is a simple featureless silhouette; room, props and Foley
  remain visibly/audibly synthetic.
- Stickman legacy faces now enlarge eyes for `brow > 1` and draw an O-mouth for
  `mouth < -0.65`; neutral and mild expressions are unchanged.
