# The Last Loaf

At four in the morning, Ben unlocked the bakery and switched on the lights. The kitchen was cold and quiet.

He tied his apron, opened the oven and slid the first tray of bread inside. While the bread baked, he sat on a stool and read yesterday's newspaper.

Someone knocked on the front door. Ben stood up and walked to the shop. A small girl in a red coat was waiting outside.

"Are you open?" she asked. Ben shook his head, then smiled and opened the door anyway.

The girl pointed at the window. "My grandmother used to buy that bread every Sunday." Ben picked up the last loaf from the shelf and gave it to her.

She held the warm bread with both hands and ran out into the street. Ben watched her until she turned the corner.

When he went back to the kitchen, there was a coin on the table. It was old, heavy, and dated 1952.
